Frequently Asked Questions

Does PASA Sentinel require root access?

No. PASA is engineered purely using standard Android Enterprise Device Administrator and Camera2/Location APIs. All capabilities—including silent snapshots, ambient audio, fake shutdown, and offline cellular SMS—function on 100% stock, unrooted Android.

How does the Offline Cellular SMS fallback operate?

If an adversary turns off Wi-Fi and Cellular Mobile Data, or enters an underground area without data connectivity, you can text PASA <masterPassword> /locate from any phone. PASA's background broadcast receiver intercepts this incoming PDU, triangulates GPS, and returns an encrypted reverse SMS with exact Google Maps coordinates.

Can a thief simply uninstall the app from the settings menu?

When armed as an Android Device Administrator, the standard Android OS "Uninstall" button is disabled by the operating system kernel. When provisioned via ADB Device Owner (dpm set-device-owner), uninstallation is strictly forbidden even in Safe Mode.

Why does Google Play Protect display a warning during sideloading?

Google Play Protect alerts users for off-market APKs that are not indexed in Google's commercial database. Because PASA is an intentional sovereign defense tool that refuses to submit customer telemetry to Google, it is distributed independently. Clicking "More details" → "Install anyway" resolves this instantly.
