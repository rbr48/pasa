Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Recovery

Can a thief bypass PASA by factory-resetting from Recovery Mode?

No. As Device Owner, PASA enforces hardware-level Enterprise Device Policies — ADB wipe commands are rejected without cryptographic authentication, and Factory Reset Protection ties partition access to your credentials.
Cellular

What happens if the thief pulls my SIM card?

A dedicated SIM-state receiver locks the phone the instant the tray is ejected and caches a GPS fix. When any replacement SIM is inserted, PASA silently SMS's the new IMSI, number and coordinates to your trusted contact.
Airplane mode

Can a thief just toggle Airplane Mode from Quick Settings?

No. Device Owner policy freezes the Quick Settings shade on the lock screen — Wi-Fi, mobile data and airplane mode can't be toggled without your master PIN. Local alarms and offline SMS keep running regardless.
Covert trap

How does the Fake Shutdown Blackout work?

Holding the power button triggers a spoofed shutdown animation and a black screen — indistinguishable from a real power-off. The background daemon keeps GPS, microphone and the Telegram relay running the entire time.
Privacy

Does PASA send any data to your servers?

No. There is no central telemetry backend. Location and evidence are delivered directly to the Telegram bot and/or phone number you configure — we never see or store that data.
Licensing

Is this really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pro and Fleet tiers are single, lifetime purchases that include all future OTA updates for your licensed device count — no recurring subscription.
