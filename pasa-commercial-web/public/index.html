Frequently Asked Questions

Why not use Google Find My Device?
Google's solution operates in the user space. A thief can simply turn on Airplane Mode, power down the device, or factory reset it from recovery. PASA operates as a Device Owner, meaning it blocks the power menu (fake shutdown), blocks Airplane Mode, severs USB data connections, and survives standard resets.
Does this require a Rooted device?
Zero Root required. PASA leverages standard Android Enterprise API capabilities via a one-time ADB setup process to grant itself Device Owner privileges.
Is it available on Google Play?
No. Google strictly bans apps with hardware-level lockdown capabilities and background C2 operations from the Play Store. PASA is distributed exclusively as a sovereign sideloaded APK.
What happens after the trial expires?
If you do not purchase a Pro license, all remote commands lock down except for /license, /help, and /info. The app will remain on your device but defensive operations will cease until a license is applied.
How is evidence delivered if you have zero cloud storage?
When a trap triggers or a command is issued, the device processes the data in RAM, encrypts the payload, and POSTs it directly to Telegram's API via your private Bot Token. It then instantly shreds the local RAM buffer. Our infrastructure never touches the data.
