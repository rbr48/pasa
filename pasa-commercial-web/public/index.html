Frequently Asked Questions

Recovery Protection

Can a thief bypass PASA Sentinel by factory resetting from Recovery Mode?

No. When provisioned as Android Device Owner via ADB, PASA Sentinel enforces hardware-level Enterprise Device Policies. USB debugging remains persistently armed, ADB wipe commands are rejected without cryptographic authentication, and Android Factory Reset Protection (FRP) permanently ties hardware partition access to your credentials. Even if wiped via custom hardware flash boxes, the IMEI and carrier locks remain permanently registered in international theft databases.

Cellular PDU

How does offline defense work if the thief removes my SIM card?

PASA Sentinel includes a dedicated hardware SIM State Broadcast Receiver. As soon as the tray is ejected, the phone instantly locks down, silences notifications, and caches high-precision GNSS fixes. Whenever any replacement SIM card is inserted by the thief or resale shop, PASA automatically and silently transmits an encrypted SMS containing the new IMSI, phone number, and GPS coordinates to your predefined trusted emergency contact without alerting the thief.

Airplane Mode Lock

Can a thief simply turn on Airplane Mode or pull down Quick Settings?

No. Through Knox-Grade Device Owner policies, PASA Sentinel can freeze and disable the pull-down Quick Settings shade and status bar on the lock screen. The thief cannot toggle Airplane Mode, turn off Wi-Fi, or disable Mobile Data without first entering your master cryptographic PIN. Furthermore, kinetic snatch alarms, local forensic photo capture, and offline SMS fallback continue running uninterrupted regardless of network connectivity.

Covert Trap

How does the Fake Shutdown Blackout feature work in real life?

When a thief long-presses the power button or taps 'Power Off' (or when a remote /fakeshutdown command is dispatched), PASA intercepts the power intent, plays a realistic spoofed Android shutdown animation, and immediately drives display panel illumination to 0 nits. To the thief, the phone feels completely switched off. In reality, the daemon stays running in low-power standby — buffering microphone audio, streaming GPS coordinates, and capturing covert mugshots whenever the phone is picked up.

Anti-Tamper

Can PASA Sentinel be uninstalled by a thief or suspicious third party?

Impossible without your Master PIN. When provisioned as Android Device Owner via ADB, the "Uninstall" and "Force Stop" buttons are completely grayed out in Android System Settings. Even in Safe Mode, Device Owner policies remain strictly enforced by the Android OS framework. De-provisioning requires entering your cryptographic Master PIN inside the app or executing an authenticated remote unbind command.

Zero Root

Is root access or bootloader unlocking required to run PASA Sentinel?

No root required whatsoever. PASA Sentinel utilizes standard, official Android Enterprise Device Administration and Device Policy Manager (DPM) APIs. You do not need to root your smartphone, flash custom recoveries (TWRP), or unlock the bootloader. Your manufacturer hardware warranty remains 100% valid, and security chips (Samsung Knox, Google Titan M2) remain completely intact.

Battery Profile

Will background motion sensors and Telegram long-polling drain my battery?

Battery impact is negligible — typically less than 1.2% over a full 24-hour cycle.
