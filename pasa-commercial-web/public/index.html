Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers on device persistence, offline SMS, privacy architecture, and guarantees.

Can a thief bypass PASA Sentinel by factory resetting from Recovery Mode?

No. When provisioned as Android Device Owner via ADB, PASA enforces hardware-level Enterprise Device Policies. USB debugging remains armed, ADB wipe commands are rejected without cryptographic authentication, and Android Factory Reset Protection (FRP) permanently ties hardware partition access to your credentials. Even if wiped via custom hardware boxes, the IMEI and carrier locks remain active.

How does offline defense work if the thief removes my SIM card?

PASA Sentinel includes a dedicated hardware SIM State Broadcast Receiver. As soon as the tray is ejected, the phone locks down, silences notification sounds, and caches GPS fixes. Whenever any new SIM card is inserted by the thief or resale shop, PASA automatically and silently transmits an encrypted SMS containing the new IMSI, phone number, and GPS coordinates to your predefined trusted emergency contact.

Will background motion sensors and Telegram long-polling drain my battery?

Battery impact is negligible — typically less than 1.2% over a 24-hour cycle. PASA utilizes Android hardware sensor batching (low-power DSP) and event-driven C2 sockets rather than continuous CPU wake-locks. The kinetic snatch trigger uses a low-frequency hardware accelerometer interrupt that only wakes the main processor when a high-G acceleration delta is verified.
