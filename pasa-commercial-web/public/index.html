Clarity & Architecture

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical details on device requirements, installation nuances, and privacy guarantees.

Google and Samsung trackers operate in standard user space and depend entirely on active internet connectivity. In real-world theft, thieves toggle Airplane Mode or pull out the SIM card within 5 to 10 seconds, rendering Google and Samsung completely blind. Furthermore, they immediately power off the device. PASA Sentinel operates as Android Enterprise Device Owner: it blocks the status bar pull-down (setStatusBarDisabled), disables Airplane Mode toggling, fakes OEM shutdown animations while keeping GPS and cameras active on a 0-nit black canvas, and provides air-gapped cellular SMS C2 fallback even with data turned off.
Zero Root required. In fact, rooting degrades security by tripping hardware Knox counters and disabling TEE / StrongBox Keystore encryption. PASA utilizes Google's official Android Enterprise Device Owner specification (DevicePolicyManager). It is configured once via USB ADB or Zero-Touch QR, attaining supervisor administrative authority without altering kernel partitions or voiding hardware integrity.
Google Play Store Developer Policies strictly prohibit third-party apps from utilizing hardware-level defensive APIs, including: severing physical USB data pins, executing remote hardware lockscreen PIN resets via escrow tokens, headlessly capturing background photos without screen overlays, and disabling the device uninstallation mechanism. PASA is distributed exclusively as a sovereign sideloaded APK to provide uncompromised hardware defense.
When a trap triggers or a remote command is executed, evidence (mugshot photo, ambient audio, or GPS fix) is processed in volatile device RAM and transmitted directly to the official Telegram Bot API via TLS 1.3 to your private bot. The moment transmission concludes, the RAM buffer and temporary file are cryptographically shredded on the phone. Our control plane servers never store photos, recordings, or GPS coordinates on disk.
PASA Sentinel operates on a strict 7-day hard lockout policy. During the trial, all 98+ features are operational with a countdown banner displayed in Telegram. At exactly day 7, all security commands lock down, leaving only /license, /help, and /info functional until a Pro Lifetime or Enterprise key is activated.
