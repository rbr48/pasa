Google and Samsung trackers operate in ordinary user space and depend entirely on active internet connectivity. A thief immediately turns on Airplane Mode or removes the SIM card within 5–10 seconds, permanently blinding Google. PASA operates as Knox Device Owner: it blocks status bar pull-down, disables Airplane Mode toggling, fakes OEM shutdown animations while keeping GPS and cameras active, and provides air-gapped cellular SMS fallback even without mobile data.
