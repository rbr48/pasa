PASA Sentinel equips the verified administrator with powerful, hardware-grade countermeasures intended to protect confidentiality in extreme compromise scenarios:

Remote Factory Reset /wipe

Irreversibly triggers a low-level physical sanitization of the device storage, destroying all accounts, photos, messages, keys, and operating system state.
